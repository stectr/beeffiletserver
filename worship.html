Song sheet editor

Song sheet Click a word to edit it, or the space above it to add a chord. Shift+Enter starts a new page.

Hover over a word to highlight it, then click above it to enter a chord or click the word to edit it. Click at the end of a line to add words, or anywhere on an empty line to start typing; Enter starts a new line and pasting adds whole lyrics. Tab moves through chords and pauses; Shift+Tab moves back. Delete chord text to remove it. Shift+Enter starts a new page. Type the song title on the first line and add its key in brackets, like As the Deer (G), and the key shows in bold italics.

Formatting and page tips

Lines starting with Verse, Chorus, Bridge, or ( are bold section cues. For an instrumental line, include bar lines, e.g. Intro: | G / / / | A / / / |.
