Chord Sheet Generator v1.67

Formatting and page tips
  • Lyrics: Click a word or anywhere on an empty line to type; paste lyrics to add multiple lines. Click unused space in the upper or lower half of a row to edit its last chord or lyric. Drag across lyrics or from the paper margin to select and copy them with their chords. Left and right arrows, including Ctrl or Cmd+arrow, move between boxes at their edges. Press Enter for a new line; Ctrl+A twice (Cmd+A on Mac) selects the whole sheet.
  • Chords: Click above a word to add a chord. A hyphen splits a word into chord blocks, such as melo-dious. Left and right arrows, including Ctrl or Cmd+arrow, move through a chord and between boxes at their edges; up and down move between lines. Enter moves the word under the chord to a new line; Shift+Enter starts a new page. Tab moves chord text after the cursor forward; Shift+Tab before a chord moves it back only if the previous block is empty. Backspace at the start moves it back and combines with the previous chord, or navigates back if the block is empty. Ctrl or Cmd+Backspace in an empty block jumps to the previous non-empty chord. Drag across chords to copy them; pasted chords keep their lines, skipping section headings and adding blocks when needed.
  • Title and key: Add the key in parentheses, such as Song title (G), for a compact bold italic title.
  • Sections: Lines starting with Verse, Chorus, Bridge, or ( are bold and have no chord slots.
  • Labels and instrumental lines: Start a line with a word and colon, such as Instrumental:, for a line without chord slots. The label is bold; Intro: immediately after a title stays compact. Time signatures like 4/4 display vertically.
  • Pages and saving: Shift+Enter starts a new page; Backspace on an empty line after a page break removes it. Click a preview on the right to jump to a page; drag previews or use their arrows to rearrange pages. On a narrow or zoomed-in screen, scroll horizontally to reach the sidebar. Use Print or Ctrl/Cmd+P to print or save a PDF. Your sheet saves on this device; Export and Import share editable .chords.json files. Import can replace the sheet or add it on a new page.
